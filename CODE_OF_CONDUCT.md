# Code of conduct

Help keep Uptivra welcoming and useful. Treat contributors with respect, discuss the work rather than attacking people, and give clear, constructive feedback. Respect privacy and differing levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing and deliberate disruption are unacceptable in project issues, reviews and other project spaces. Do not publish another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit/remove contributions or restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate and based on conduct, not identity or disagreement.

Contact the [maintainer](https://github.com/timmsy1998) to arrange a private channel for conduct concerns; avoid posting sensitive details publicly. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
